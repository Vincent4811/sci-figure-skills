# Journal figure conventions (verified 2026-10-31; always re-check the target journal's Guide for Authors)

| Item | Nature (research figure guide) | Cell Press | PLOS | Elsevier (generic artwork) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Panel labels | 8 pt **bold, upright, lowercase** a, b, c | **Uppercase** A, B, C (bold in practice) | Not prescribed centrally; uppercase common; captions not in image | Journal-specific |
| Font | Sans-serif, Arial/Helvetica; Courier for sequences; Symbol for Greek | Arial or Helvetica only, embedded | Arial, Times, or Symbol only | Arial, Courier, Symbol, Times (Arial preferred over Helvetica variants) |
| Text size | 5–7 pt (labels 8 pt) | ~6–8 pt (secondary sources) | 8–12 pt | ~7 pt; sub/superscripts ≥6 pt |
| Widths | 89 mm single / 183 mm double (Nature house layout) | 85 / 114 / 174 mm; fit on 8.5×11 in page | 789–2250 px @300 dpi (6.68–19.05 cm); max height 2625 px (22.23 cm) | 90 / 140 / 190 mm; min 30 mm |
| Lines | – | 0.35–1.5 pt, ≥0.5 pt | – | 0.10–1.5 pt |
| Resolution | photos ≥300 dpi (450 dpi best) | colour 300, B/W 500, line art 1000 dpi | 300–600 dpi | halftone 300, combination 500, line art 1000 dpi |
| Format | vector PDF/EPS, editable text, fonts embedded (Type 2/42), RGB | TIFF/PDF, RGB, fonts embedded | TIFF (LZW) or EPS, <10 MB | TIFF/EPS/PDF, fonts embedded |
| Other | no gridlines, no coloured text, accessible palette, axes with units | individual data points when error bars shown; scale bars | no figure titles/captions inside image | uniform look across article |
