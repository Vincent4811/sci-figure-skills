# Elsevier: figure specifications

Last checked: 2026-11-01. ✅ verified at the cited source (the official artwork instructions PDF, an older edition); ⚠️ check before use.

## Publisher base rules (generic artwork instructions)
| Item | Rule | Source / status |
|---|---|---|
| Font | Arial, Courier, Symbol, or Times. Arial is preferred over Helvetica variants; one older edition also lists Helvetica. | ✅ [ELS-2009], [ELS-old] |
| Font size | About 7 pt finished size; subscripts and superscripts at least 6 pt; a rule of thumb, sometimes up to ~10 pt | ✅ [ELS-old] |
| Panel label style | Not set centrally; depends on the journal | ⚠️ check the journal's Guide for Authors |
| Column widths | Minimum 30 mm; single ~90 mm; 1.5 columns ~140 mm; full width ~190 mm | ✅ [ELS-2009] |
| Max figure dimensions | Height up to about 240 mm | ⚠️ secondary [LEHUYNH]; the 2009 PDF shows a 240 mm figure |
| File formats | TIFF, EPS or PDF; fonts embedded | ⚠️ secondary [SCIPLOT] |
| Resolution | Halftone 300 dpi; combination art 500 dpi; line art 1,000 dpi (1,200 dpi for very fine line art) | ✅ [ELS-2009]; 1,200 dpi ⚠️ [SCIPLOT] |
| Colour mode | RGB unless the journal says otherwise | ⚠️ secondary [SCIPLOT] |
| Line weights | 0.10–1.5 pt | ⚠️ secondary [SCIPLOT] |
| Other | Uniform look across all artwork in one article | ✅ [ELS-old] |
| Graphical abstract | At least 1,328 px wide × 531 px high | ⚠️ from memory; check the Elsevier GA page |

## Journal exceptions
No journal-specific exceptions have been verified yet. Each Elsevier journal's Guide for Authors overrides this baseline. Add exceptions here, for example for *Food Microbiology* or *International Journal of Food Microbiology*, after checking their guides. ⚠️
