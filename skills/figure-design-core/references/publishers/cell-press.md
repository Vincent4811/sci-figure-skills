# Cell Press: figure specifications

Last checked: 2026-11-01. ✅ verified at the cited source; ⚠️ secondary source, so check before use. The official Cell Press figure-guidelines page was not fetched directly; its text was taken from quoted copies.

## Publisher base rules
| Item | Rule | Source / status |
|---|---|---|
| Font | Arial or Helvetica only, embedded | ⚠️ guideline as quoted [CNS]; Cell Reports summary says Arial only [MANU] |
| Font size | About 6–8 pt | ⚠️ secondary [MANU] |
| Panel label style | **Capital letters** A, B, C, bold in practice | ⚠️ quoted [CNS]; bold and ~9–10 pt per [FG] |
| Column widths | 85 mm (1 col) / 114 mm (1.5 col) / 174 mm (2 col); 55 mm columns for Previews and other three-column formats | ⚠️ quoted [CNS] |
| Max figure dimensions | Each figure fits on one 8.5 × 11 in page | ✅ [CS-checklist]; ⚠️ [FG] says max height 225 mm |
| File formats | TIFF or PDF preferred, also JPEG or EPS. PDF < 3 MB at initial submission; each final file ≤ 20 MB. All panels in one file. | ⚠️ quoted [CNS]; ✅ separate high-res TIFF/PDF per figure [CS-checklist] |
| Resolution | Colour 300 dpi; black and white 500 dpi; line art 1,000 dpi | ⚠️ quoted [CNS] |
| Colour mode | RGB | ⚠️ quoted [CNS] |
| Line weights | 0.35–1.5 pt; never narrower than 0.5 pt | ⚠️ quoted [CNS] |
| Grey fills | Neighbouring fills differ by at least 20%; between 10% and 80% grey | ⚠️ quoted [CNS] |
| Other | Show individual data points with pooled data and error bars; scale bars on micrographs; legend starts with a title | ✅ [CS-checklist] |
| Graphical abstract | Single panel, no text below 7 pt, landscape | ⚠️ secondary [FG]; check before use |

## Journal exceptions

### Cell Systems
| Item | Differs how | Source / status |
|---|---|---|
| Display items | Article, Synthesis or Methods: 7; Report: 4; Brief Report: 2 | ✅ [CS-checklist] |

### Cell Reports
| Item | Differs how | Source / status |
|---|---|---|
| Font | Arial only, about 6–8 pt | ⚠️ secondary [MANU] |
| Display items | 7 for Research articles and Resources; 4 for Reports | ⚠️ secondary [MANU] |

### Width discrepancy
One secondary source [FG] gives 85 / 170 mm. The quoted official guideline gives 174 mm. **Use 174 mm unless the journal says otherwise.** ⚠️
